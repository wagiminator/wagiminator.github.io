Power Profiler

The current of a device from nanoamperes to an ampere, 100 000 times a second, with the Nordic Power Profiler Kit II on USB. Supply the device or measure in its supply line, watch the current live together with up to eight logic signals, and sum up any stretch: average, peak, charge and energy. Without a PPK2 the simulator shows how it works.

Good to know

  • Browser: USB access with Web Serial works in Chrome, Edge and Opera on the desktop. On Linux the user needs access to the serial port (group dialout or a udev rule). Use the USB connector of the PPK2 marked DATA/POWER.
  • Source meter: the PPK2 supplies the device with 0.8 to 5 V at up to 1 A and measures its current; connect it to VOUT and GND. Ampere meter: the PPK2 sits in the supply line of the device (VIN from the supply, VOUT to the device); enter the voltage of that supply for the energy.
  • Logic inputs: D0–D7 of the PPK2 show what the firmware is doing, for example a pin set while the radio sends. Connect the logic port's VCC to the logic level of the device.
  • Chart: black or white background (white for printing and screenshots); the light band shows the lowest and highest value of each pixel column, the line the average. Wheel or pinch zooms, dragging selects a stretch (or moves the view in Move mode), double click zooms into the selection, the bar below moves through the whole recording. Keys: ←/→, + and −, F, Esc.
  • Long recordings: 100 000 samples per second take 0.5 MB per second of memory. For hours, choose fewer samples per second: each sample is then the average of the 10 µs samples.
  • Recordings are saved as .pprec (compressed) and can be opened again here; CSV gives time, current and the logic inputs for spreadsheets.
