Interactive BOM

For soldering a board by hand: the bill of materials next to a picture of the board made from its Gerber files. Choose a line and its parts light up on the board; tick them off as you go. Works with the files of EasyEDA, KiCad and JLCPCB, and with the projects on GitHub.

Good to know

  • Files: the Gerber files (as ZIP or single files), the BOM (EasyEDA .tsv, KiCad .csv, JLCPCB .csv) and the pick-and-place file (EasyEDA, JLCPCB CPL, KiCad .pos/.csv, Eagle .mnt). Everything is read in the browser; nothing is uploaded.
  • Without pick-and-place file the app does not know where the parts are. Switch on Teach positions and click each part once on the board; save the result as CPL file next to the BOM, and the next time it is found by itself.
  • GitHub: the app looks for Gerber ZIP, BOM and CPL files in the repository. Links like ?repo=wagiminator/CH552-USB-OLED open a project directly. For the boards of wagiminator's projects the part positions come with the app; they are found by the EasyEDA id in the Gerber files, also when you open the files from your computer.
  • Soldering: choose a line (or press N for the next one), its parts light up. Tick the line when it is done; the progress is kept in this browser for each board. “Small parts first” is a good order for hand soldering.
  • View: wheel or pinch zooms, dragging moves, double click zooms in. Click a part on the board to find its line. Keys: ↑/↓ choose the line, Space ticks it, T and B switch the side.
  • Download: the Gerber ZIP, the BOM and the part positions as CPL file (JLCPCB format, with the taught positions). A4 sheet makes a PDF with the board picture, the parts numbered by line and the parts list with boxes to tick, for the workbench without a computer; it uses the order chosen above the list.
  • The board picture is drawn by the same code as the Gerber Viewer.
