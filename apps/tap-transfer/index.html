C64 Tape Transfer

Read a cassette from the Datasette into a TAP file, write a TAP file back to tape, look at every pulse and clean up worn recordings. Works with the TapeBuddy64, TapeDump64 (read only) and DumpMaster64.

This browser cannot talk to USB adapters. You can still open, inspect and optimize TAP files; for reading and writing tapes use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

Rewind the cassette, choose Read tape, then press PLAY on the Datasette within 10 seconds. Reading ends when you press STOP, at the end of the tape or after 25 seconds without signal.

Open or read a TAP file first; it is written exactly as shown in the pulse view.

Version to write

Choose Write to tape, then press RECORD and PLAY on the Datasette within 10 seconds.
