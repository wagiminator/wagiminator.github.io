USB Oscilloscope

The cheap two-channel USB oscilloscopes Hantek 6022BE and 6022BL (and the DSO-6021, Voltcraft DSO-2020 and SainSmart DDS120) in the browser, without installing anything: up to 30 million samples per second, trigger, measurements, cursors, spectrum and XY mode. The open source firmware is loaded into the scope when it is connected.

Good to know

  • Browser and driver: WebUSB works in Chrome, Edge and Opera on the desktop. Windows needs the WinUSB driver for the scope (install it with Zadig, for the device before and after the firmware is loaded); Linux needs a udev rule for the USB IDs 04b4/04b5 and 8102, macOS nothing.
  • Connecting: choose the scope; the firmware is loaded and the scope comes back as an "OpenHantek" device. If the browser asks again, choose it once more. The firmware is the open source firmware of Hantek6022API (GPL), as used by OpenHantek6022; see the notes on the firmware.
  • Ranges: 20 mV to 5 V per division with a 1:1 probe (the input takes ±5 V; with the probe at 10:1, ±50 V). Set the probe factor to the switch on the probe. Over range shows a warning at the channel.
  • AC coupling works on the DDS120 and on Hantek scopes with the small AC/DC modification; otherwise the inputs are always DC.
  • Sample rate: chosen from the time base, up to 30 MS/s with one channel and 15 MS/s with two (USB 2.0 is the limit). The analog bandwidth of the 6022BE is about 20 MHz.
  • Trigger: Auto shows the signal also without a trigger, Normal waits, Single stops after one trigger. From 100 ms/div the display rolls. Drag the markers on the screen: zero lines (left), trigger level (right), trigger point (top).
  • Calibration: the 6022BE/BL and DSO-6021 carry offset and gain values in their EEPROM, which are used. Zero offsets measures the offsets of all ranges again with the inputs connected to ground and keeps them in this browser.
  • Measurements use the whole screen: frequency and duty cycle need at least two periods on it.
