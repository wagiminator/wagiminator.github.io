AVR Tiny UPDI Programmer

Program tinyAVR 0-, 1- and 2-series microcontrollers (ATtiny202 to ATtiny3227) with a plain USB-to-serial converter on their one-wire UPDI pin. Write BIN and HEX files, erase the chip, set the fuses and choose the 16 or 20 MHz oscillator.

This browser cannot access serial ports. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

Firmware

    Choose Flash firmware and select the converter's serial port. The chip is erased, the firmware written and verified, and the chip restarts with it.

    Clock and fuses

    Selects the 16 or 20 MHz internal oscillator with its factory calibration (fuse OSCCFG). The firmware divides it down to the chosen clock with the prescaler, so build it for the same frequency (F_CPU).

    #FuseChipNewMeaning

    Enter new values as hex (for example 0x04). Empty fields stay as they are. Careful with SYSCFG0: if its reset pin setting is not UPDI, the chip can only be programmed again with a 12 V high-voltage programmer.

    Chip

    Shows chip type, flash size, revision, serial number and fuses. Changes nothing.

    Erases flash and EEPROM (unless SYSCFG0 keeps the EEPROM). Also unlocks a locked chip.

    Wiring

    UPDI needs only one data wire. Join TXD and RXD of the converter through a resistor and connect RXD to the UPDI pin of the chip:

    Converter              tinyAVR
   TXD ---[ 1k ]---+
   RXD ------------+---> UPDI
   VCC ----------------> VCC
   GND ----------------> GND

    Any resistor from 470 Ω to 1 kΩ works, as does a Schottky diode with its cathode towards TXD. The converter and the chip must use the same voltage (3.3 V or 5 V). Boards with a built-in USB-to-UPDI circuit connect directly by USB.
