C64 SID Tracker

Compose music for the SID chip of the C64. Write notes into patterns for the three voices, line the patterns up in the order list and shape the sound with instruments. The song plays on an emulated C64 and is saved as a SID file, as a C64 program or as a sound file.

    Good to know

    Writing notes

    • Click into the pattern and play notes on the computer keyboard like on a piano: Z S X D C … for one octave, Q 2 W 3 E … for the octave above. A or 1 releases the note (===).
    • In the other columns type hexadecimal digits (0–9, A–F): the instrument, the command and its parameter. Delete clears, Insert and Backspace move the rows below.
    • Move with the arrow keys, Tab to the next voice, Page Up / Page Down. Shift with the arrows selects; Ctrl+C, X, V copy, cut and paste.
    • Space plays from the cursor or stops, Esc stops. [ and ] choose the instrument, * and / the octave.
    • The song is kept in this browser. Save it as a file to keep it for good. Changes made while the song plays are heard from the next row on.

    Commands

    1xxSlide up by xx per frame, in its rows
    2xxSlide down by xx per frame, in its rows
    3xxSlide to the note of the row, speed xx
    4xyVibrato: speed x, depth y, until the next note
    5xyAttack x, decay y
    6xySustain x, release y
    7xxWaveform (control register), stops the wave program
    8xyArpeggio: note, +x, +y semitones, until the next note
    9xxPulse width xx × 16
    AxxFilter cutoff
    BxyFilter resonance x, type y (1 low, 2 band, 4 high pass)
    CxxVolume (0–F)
    DxxFilter sweep per frame (80–FF go down)
    FxxSpeed: frames per row
