PUYA ISP Programmer

Flash PUYA PY32 microcontrollers (PY32F002A/B, PY32F003, PY32F030, PY32F07x, PY32F040) with a cheap USB-to-serial converter and the bootloader built into every chip. Write BIN and HEX files, erase, lock and unlock the chip and set the nRST pin.

This browser cannot access serial ports. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

Firmware

    Put the chip into bootloader mode, choose Flash firmware and select the converter's serial port. The chip is erased, the firmware written and verified byte by byte, and the chip starts it.

    Wiring and bootloader mode

    Connect the converter to the chip (3.3 V or 5 V, as the chip is powered):

    Converter   PY32F0xx
   RXD  <-- PA2 or PA9  or PA14
   TXD  --> PA3 or PA10 or PA15
   VCC  --> VCC
   GND  --> GND

    Then start the bootloader in ONE of these ways:

    • While powering up: unplug the converter, pull BOOT0 (PF4) to VCC or hold the BOOT button, plug the converter in and release BOOT0.
    • With a reset: pull BOOT0 (PF4) to VCC, briefly pull nRST (PF2) to GND, release BOOT0 (or hold BOOT, press and release RESET, release BOOT).
    • Automatically: with a CH340X converter whose control lines are wired to nRST and BOOT0, the app starts the bootloader by itself and resets the chip afterwards.
