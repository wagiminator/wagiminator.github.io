      Good to know

      • PSID tunes are run like SIDPLAY does: the player calls their init and play routines. RSID tunes run in a full C64 environment with their own interrupts (also digi tunes and BASIC programs). Tunes for two or three SIDs are supported.
      • The oscilloscopes show each voice before volume and filter, so muted voices stay visible. Click M to mute a voice and S to hear it alone.
      • The tracker log shows one row per call of the play routine (or per frame for RSID tunes): new notes, waveform (control register), envelope, pulse width and filter.
      • Keys: Space play/pause, ← → song, ↑ ↓ tune, 1–9 mute a voice, 0 all voices on.
      • The C64 runs on the free Open ROMs. Tunes that need the original KERNAL routines may not play.
