Power Analyzer

The control program for the ATtiny814 Power Analyzer, an electronic load with two power sensors: live readings, a constant current load and the automatic tests of power supplies, DC/DC converters and batteries, with charts that can be compared and saved.

This browser cannot reach serial ports. Open the page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer; saved measurements and CSV files can be viewed anywhere.

Good to know

      

        
    
          
  • The analyzer takes up to 26 V, 5 A and 25 W on the load. It switches the load off by itself when the power or the heat sink temperature is too high. Use a good heat sink with a fan.
    • 
          
  • All terminals and USB share one ground. A USB isolator between the analyzer and the computer prevents ground loops.
    • 
          
  • The load cannot go below about 17 mA. Load, efficiency and ripple tests stop by themselves at the maximum current or the stop voltage; the battery test and the multimeter can be stopped at any time.
    • 
        

        
    
          
  • The CH330N/CH340N needs no driver on current Windows, macOS and Linux versions; on Linux the user must be in the dialout group. Close other serial monitors first.
    • 
          
  • Measurements are kept in this browser. Save them as CSV files to keep them for good; CSV files of the Python program can be opened too.
    • 
          
  • The simulator behaves like the firmware with a USB charger on TEST-IN, a 12 V to 5 V converter for the efficiency test and a lithium-ion cell for the battery test (which runs much faster than real time).
