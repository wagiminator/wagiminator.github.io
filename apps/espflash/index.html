ESP32/ESP8266 Flasher

Flash Espressif chips through their serial download mode, as esptool does: the ESP32, ESP32-S2, -S3, -C2, -C3, -C5, -C6, -C61, -H2, -P4 and the ESP8266. Through a USB-to-serial converter or the built-in USB port of the newer chips, with automatic reset on most boards. Bootloader, partition table and program go to their addresses and are checked afterwards.

This browser cannot access serial ports. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

    Download mode

    • Development boards (DevKit, NodeMCU, Wemos D1 and most others) have an auto-reset circuit: DTR and RTS of the converter pull IO0 (GPIO0) and EN, so the app starts the download mode by itself.
    • Built-in USB of the ESP32-C3, -C5, -C6, -C61, -H2, -P4 and -S3 (USB-Serial/JTAG): connect the chip's USB pins directly; the reset works the same way through the USB port.
    • By hand: hold BOOT (GPIO0 on the ESP32, -S2, -S3 and ESP8266, GPIO9 on most C- and H-series chips), press and release RESET (EN), release BOOT. Then choose “Reset: by hand” under More options.
    • ESP32-S2 with native USB: enter the download mode by hand; the chip then appears as a new serial port. Press RESET after flashing.

    Bare modules need a 3.3 V converter: TXD to RXD0 (U0RXD), RXD to TXD0 (U0TXD), GND to GND, and a supply that can deliver at least 300 mA.
