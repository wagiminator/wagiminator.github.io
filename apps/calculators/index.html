Electronics Calculators

Small calculators for building the boards: regulators and chargers, resistors and LEDs, the timers and serial ports of microcontrollers, traces and DC/DC converters. Values can be typed like 4k7, 4.7k, 100n or 2u2; the results follow as you type.

Feedback divider

Output voltage of an adjustable regulator: Vout = Vref · (1 + Rtop / Rbottom). Rtop goes from the output to FB, Rbottom from FB to ground.

Linear regulator heat

A linear regulator (78xx, AMS1117, LM317, LDO) turns the voltage difference into heat: P = (Vin − Vout) · I.

θJA depends on the copper around the part; the presets are typical datasheet values.

Battery runtime

Average current of a device that sleeps most of the time, how long a battery lasts and how long it charges.

LED series resistor

R = (Vsupply − n · Vf) / I, rounded up to the next value of the series.

Modern LEDs are bright at 1–5 mA; 20 mA is the rating, not the target.

LED driver current

Constant current LED drivers regulate the voltage across a sense resistor: I = VFB / Rsense.

Voltage divider

Vout = Vin · Rbottom / (Rtop + Rbottom), for example to measure a battery with the ADC.

SMD resistor code

3-digit, 4-digit and EIA-96 codes printed on SMD resistors, both ways.

EIA-96: two digits for the value (01 = 100 … 96 = 976) and a letter for the multiplier (Z ×0.001, Y/R ×0.01, X/S ×0.1, A ×1, B/H ×10, C ×100, D ×1 k, E ×10 k, F ×100 k). R marks the decimal point: 4R7 = 4.7 Ω.

Timer and PWM frequency

Prescaler and top value for a frequency, with the resolution of the PWM.

Cycles and time

Clock cycles of a delay loop or an interrupt routine as time, and back.

UART baud rate

Register value, real baud rate and error for a clock; up to about ±2 % works.

Trace width

Width of a copper trace for a current after IPC-2221, with resistance and voltage drop.

Via current

Current of a plated via after IPC-2221 (the barrel as conductor), and how many vias a current needs.

Clearance

Minimum spacing between conductors for a voltage after IPC-2221 (table 6-1).

Up to 3050 m above sea level. For mains voltages the safety standards of the device (creepage distances, insulation) apply and ask for more; 230 V AC has a peak of 325 V.

DC/DC converter

Duty cycle, inductor and capacitors of a buck, boost or SEPIC converter, as a starting point before the datasheet.

Good to know

  • Each calculator has its own link, for example #feedback for the feedback divider (with the MT3608 of the Power Boards), #uart or #trace. The values you typed are kept in this browser.
  • The results are calculated values for the design; check the limits in the datasheet of the part (maximum currents, voltages and power).
