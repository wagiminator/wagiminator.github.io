Draw graphics for the C64: sprites, sprite objects made of several sprites and animation frames, character sets, tiles and game levels built from them, and multicolour bitmaps. Pictures can be converted to C64 graphics. Everything is saved in C64 formats for your programs.

      Good to know

      • Drawing: left mouse button paints with the first colour, right button with the second. Tools: pen (P), line (L), rectangle (R), filled rectangle (B), ellipse (E), fill (F) and pick a colour (K, or Alt+click). Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
      • Objects are made of sprites: several sprites on top of each other (for example a hires outline over a multicolour sprite, or a large figure from four sprites) and several frames for an animation.
      • Levels are built from tiles, and tiles from characters. Every character has its own colour; in multicolour mode, colours 8–15 make a character multicolour, like on the C64.
      • Bitmaps follow the C64 rules: in multicolour, a 4×8 cell holds the background and three more colours. The converter turns pictures into C64 bitmaps, with or without dithering.
      • Your work is kept in this browser while you edit. Save project writes all of it to a file; Export writes sprites, characters, tiles, levels and bitmaps in C64 formats (raw data, PRG, SpritePad 1.x, Koala Painter, Art Studio), as assembler source or as PNG.
      • The C64 character set comes from the free MEGA65 Open ROMs.
