C64 Disk Transfer

Read a floppy disk into a .d64 image, write an image back to a disk or format a disk, using a 1541 or 1541-II drive and a DiskBuddy64, DiskMaster64 or DumpMaster64 adapter.

This browser cannot talk to USB serial devices. Open this page in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a desktop computer.

Copies the disk in the drive into a .d64 image that you can download or open in the Disk Editor or Disk Viewer.

Formats the disk in the drive with a fast formatter. Everything on the disk is erased.
